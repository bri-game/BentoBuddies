## Full description  (max 4000)
Pack a lunchbox. Make a friend smile.

Bento Buddies is a cozy packing puzzle about fitting food into a lunchbox — and getting it just right for the friend who opens it.

Every box is a small spatial puzzle. Slide rice, veggies, berries and cookies into place until everything fits. Then mind the little things each friend cares about: rice likes to sit beside a veggie, ice cream needs to stay away from the soup, a berry should touch the rim, and the cookie must stay tucked safely inside.

No timers. No lives. No move you cannot take back.

WHAT IS INSIDE
• 108 hand-built levels across 9 picnic trips
• Six gentle rules, introduced one at a time so nothing lands on you at once
• Locked pieces, marked seats, split boxes and awkward gaps to work around
• Undo and take-out are always free — experiment as much as you like
• A picnic album that keeps every box exactly the way you packed it
• Three friends to pack for: Momo Bunny, Honey Bear and Minty Frog

COMPLETELY OFFLINE
Bento Buddies needs no internet connection at all — it does not even ask for network permission. No ads, no accounts, no purchases, no tracking. Your progress is saved on your device and stays there.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE
The difficulty rises and falls on purpose. Every few boxes there is a relaxing one, so a hard puzzle is never followed straight away by another. Come back whenever you like — your box is waiting exactly where you left it.
